## Project Overview
This Tableau dashboard analyzes Nigeria's carbon dioxide emissions over a 22-year period, examining emission sources, trends, and per capita impact to understand the nation's environmental footprint.

**Data Source:** Global Carbon Budget  
**Tools Used:** Tableau  
**Analysis Period:** 2000-2022

## Dashboard Features & Insights

### Emission Source Breakdown
- **Oil:** 1,080 Mt (Primary contributor)
- **Flaring:** 603 Mt (Significant environmental concern)
- **Gas:** 566 Mt
- **Cement:** 105 Mt
- **Coal:** 2 Mt (Negligible contribution)

### Key Trends Identified

#### 1. Temporal Patterns
- Volatile emission peaks observed around 2005-2006
- General increasing trend until 2010-2013, followed by stabilization
- Per capita emissions remain relatively low (<0.8 tCO2/person) despite total emission fluctuations

#### 2. Source Dominance
- Fossil fuel industry (Oil, Flaring, Gas) accounts for majority of emissions
- Gas flaring represents significant wasted resource and environmental impact

### Environmental Impact & Recommendations

#### Policy Recommendations:
1. **Reduce Gas Flaring:** Implement capture and utilization technologies
2. **Diversify Energy Mix:** Invest in renewable energy infrastructure
3. **Sector-Specific Regulations:** Target oil and gas industry with emission reduction mandates

#### Research Implications:
- Investigate causes behind emission volatility (2005-2006 peak)
- Study correlation between oil production cycles and emission patterns
- Assess economic impact of flaring reduction initiatives

## Technical Implementation
- Dual-axis chart for Total vs Per Capita comparison
- Parameter controls for year selection
- Calculated fields for per capita metrics
- Interactive filters for pollutant analysis

*This analysis supports evidence-based environmental policy making and sustainable development planning for Nigeria.*
