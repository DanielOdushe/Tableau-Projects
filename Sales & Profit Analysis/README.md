## Project Overview
This Tableau dashboard analyzes sales and profit performance across time, geography, customer segments and product categories (Jan 2014 — Dec 2017). It helps identify where sales growth does not translate to profit and highlights opportunities to improve margins.

**Data Source:** Transactional orders (order date, state, customer id, category, sales, profit, discount, quantity)  
**Tools Used:** Tableau Desktop / Tableau Reader  
**Date Range:** 2014-01-03 to 2017-12-30

## Key Metrics (at a glance)
- **Total Sales:** $2,297,201  
- **Total Profit:** $286,397  
- **Profit Ratio:** 12.5%  
- **Sale per Customer:** $2,896.85  
- **Profit per Order:** 57  
- **Average Discount:** 15.62%  
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 37,873

## Major Findings
1. Overall business is profitable but margin is modest (12.5%); there is room to raise margins without losing sales.  
2. Profit volatility exists. Several months show negative profit, indicating episodic losses or return/discount effects.  
3. Geographic imbalance: some states show negative profit ratios while others carry most profits.  
4. Segment differences: Consumer and Corporate segments show steadier sales; Home Office shows smaller volume and more noise.  
5. Discounting appears material (avg. 15.6%). Investigate whether discounts drive profitable growth or erode margins.

## What the views show
- **KPI strip:** immediate health indicators for leadership.  
- **Map (Profit Ratio by State):** identifies regional winners and problem states for targeted action.  
- **Profit over Time (monthly area):** shows trend, seasonality, and months with negative profit.  
- **Sale by Segment small multiples:** channel-level performance and timing.  
- **Sale by Category small multiples:** product-category timing and profit behavior.
