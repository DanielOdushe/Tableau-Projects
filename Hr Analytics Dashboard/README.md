## Project Overview
This Tableau dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of employee attrition within an organization. The primary goal is to identify key patterns and drivers of employee turnover, enabling data-driven strategies to improve retention and organizational health.

**Data Source:** Synthetic HR Data  
**Tools Used:** Tableau  
**Key Metrics:** Attrition Rate, Employee Count, Average Age

## Dashboard Features & Insights

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **1,470** Total Employees
- **237** Attrition Count
- **16.12%** Attrition Rate (Critical Level)
- **37** Average Employee Age

### Major Findings

#### 1. Departmental Analysis
- **Sales Department Crisis:** Accounts for 96.34% of all attrition (133 employees)
- **HR Department:** Lowest attrition rate at 8.69%
- **R&D Department:** Moderate attrition at 38.82%

#### 2. Demographic Insights
- **Gender Distribution:** Male employees (150) show higher attrition than Female (87)
- **Age Vulnerability:** Employees aged 35-44 show highest attrition rates (29.11% for males)
- **Education Background:** Life Sciences (89) and Medical (93) backgrounds show highest turnover

#### 3. Job Satisfaction Analysis
- **Sales Representatives** show concerning job satisfaction scores
- **Research Scientists** and **Sales Executives** represent largest job roles
- Clear disparities in satisfaction levels across different positions

### Business Impact & Recommendations

#### Immediate Actions:
1. **Sales Department Intervention:** Conduct urgent stay interviews and review compensation structures
2. **Mid-Career Retention Program:** Target 35-44 age group with career progression opportunities
3. **Role-Specific Engagement:** Address job satisfaction issues in high-attrition roles

#### Strategic Initiatives:
- Develop specialized career paths for Life Sciences/Medical educated employees
- Implement mentorship programs for vulnerable demographic groups
- Create continuous feedback mechanisms for real-time sentiment analysis

## Technical Notes
- Utilized calculated fields for attrition rate computation
- Implemented parameters for dynamic filtering
- Used conditional formatting for highlighting critical metrics
